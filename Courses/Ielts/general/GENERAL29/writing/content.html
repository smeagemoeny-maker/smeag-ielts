WRITING TASK 1

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

A friend of yours is going to visit your city on the weekend. She sent you a letter to ask if she can spend the night at your place. Unfortunately, you live in a very small apartment and you do not have any place for her to sleep. Nonetheless, you know some good hostels she could stay at and you would be glad to meet her for a coffee.

Write a letter to your friend. In this letter
• greet her and express your happiness for her visit
• explain why she can’t spend the night at your place
• offer to meet her and to help her look for a cheap accommodation.

Write at least 150 words.

WRITING TASK 2

You should spend 40 minutes on this task.

Some people think that vegetarianism is the healthiest diet. Others believe it to be too poor on proteins.
Discuss both these views and give your opinion.

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your experience.
Write at least 250 words.
