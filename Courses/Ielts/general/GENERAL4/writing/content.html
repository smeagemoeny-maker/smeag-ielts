WRITING TASK 1


You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.


You have recently been away on holiday and while you were away your neighbour looked after your pet.

Write a letter to him/her. In your letter:
  • Thank them for their effort.
  • describe what action you will take.


You should write at least 150 words.
You do NOT need to write any addresses.
Begin your letter as follows:

Dear ________________,


You do NOT need to write your own address. Begin your letter as follows:

Dear __________

WRITING TASK 2


You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.


Write about the following topic:

Some people believe that animals should not be used in scientific experiments.
Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of using animals in scientific experiments.


Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.
